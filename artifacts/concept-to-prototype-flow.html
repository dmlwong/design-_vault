Orbit Design Brain

From a concept to a tested prototype

How a stakeholder's idea now travels through the vault — captured, gated, explored, and only then built. Two funnels: Explore diverges (fast, throwaway, not Orbit-bound); Port converges (on-system). A human owns the bet throughout.

Govern the intake
1

A concept or problem to explore

A sponsor pack, a problem statement, or a context-free Lovable prototype that today lands with no record of intent.

✎

Coach the brief into shape  ·  optional, recommended

The Brief Coach (a Claude Project) draws a strong brief out of the stakeholder through questions — it never scores or gates. Scoring is the reviewer's separate, independent job: coach and reviewer must never be the same chat (the firewall).

Fill the intake form

Capture the intent structurally: problem, one user & moment, the journey, outcomes, constraints.

tools/intake-form.html
3

Completeness check  ·  Layer 1 (a rule)

In-browser, free: required fields, real journey steps, no placeholder text. Thin brief → back to the form.

4

Commit the brief

The generated brief lands in the vault for review.

discovery/briefs/<date>-<slug>.md
5

The gate  ·  Layer 2 (judgement)

Scored against the 8-criterion brief contract. A verdict, an owner, an audit trail — reviewed as written, blind to the requester.

design-brain/brief-contract.md
Readyall eight pass → continue to Explore ↓
Needs workfixable gaps → back to the form (import → edit → resubmit)
Blockedfundamental → escalate to the owner (override logged, or stop)
↳
Context the vault lacks → Efficio Context Pack backlog. Tool/process findings → lessons inbox.
Explore · diverge
6

Build an interactive concept

A clickable, self-contained prototype — whatever best serves the idea, not Orbit-bound. Verified headless.

design-brain/skills/explore/SKILL.md
7

Blind adversarial critique

A fresh context attacks the concept, not the code: value hypothesis, assumptions, the bet, adoption, edge cases — ordered by what could kill it.

8
