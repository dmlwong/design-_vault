Efficio · OrbitWhy now

Why now

AI can now build our screens in minutes. Left untrained, it builds everyone's screens — the same generic UI every other company gets.

Speed is no longer the constraint. The constraint is making fast output ours: our tokens, our density, our platforms, our standards. That takes a source of truth machines can actually read.

Efficio · OrbitThe problem

The problem

We ran the experiment. The result wasn't Orbit.

Real test · June 2026

We asked an AI tool to build a contract-analysis screen for the client platform.

It produced a generic four-step wizard — intro card, config steps, a review screen, the result hidden behind a button. Competent, fast, and interchangeable with any product on the internet.

The tool wasn't wrong. It just had nothing of ours to learn from.

Because our design knowledge lived everywhere except somewhere readable:

  • Design tokens in the codebase
  • Component decisions in people's heads
  • Flow & UX judgment in past projects
  • Platform look & feel in screenshots
  • Accessibility standards in policy docs
Efficio · OrbitThe idea

The idea

Write it down once — in a form both people and machines obey

The Design Brain is a governed vault of contracts, not a wiki of opinions: every rule is specific enough for an AI to follow and a reviewer to enforce.

9always-on rules every tool must obey (tokens-only, platform-first, WCAG 2.2 AA…)
12component contracts — exact APIs, states, and tokens
11page patterns for full screens and flows
2platform profiles: internal Connected Platform & client-facing Orbit
13interaction & craft heuristics, each validated by blind testing
97cross-linked documents, integrity-checked on every change
