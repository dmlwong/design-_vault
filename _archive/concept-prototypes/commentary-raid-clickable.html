OwnerPriya Nair
Due date24 Jul 2026
Last updated15 Jul 2026
Description

The incumbent telematics provider has not confirmed API access for historical mileage data. Without it, the baseline spend model cannot be validated before the RFP goes to market.

Mitigation plan

Regional depots may resist a single national supplier

Change-management risk: depot managers have long-standing local supplier relationships and may push back on consolidation, slowing adoption of the awarded contract.

What we’re doing about it

Plan depot-level engagement sessions during the sourcing phase and build a transition SLA into the contract to protect service continuity.
