How to read these numbers

This badge covers three things only: the automated checks pass, nothing is overdue for review, and the review backlog isn’t growing. It is not a verdict on the vault as a whole — Storybook coverage and how much is still in review are separate measures, reported below.

142 documents in the vault; 137 carry a lifecycle status. The six cards below list 59 of them — the reusable building blocks. The other 83 are 22 benchmark records, 20 rules and foundations, 17 reviews and proposals, 7 discovery and concept briefs, and 17 indexes, templates and generated files. The component library is a separate codebase, counted separately: 52 components, 12 of which have a written spec here (23%), and 1 rendered in Storybook. Specs for the remaining 40 are the work after that.
