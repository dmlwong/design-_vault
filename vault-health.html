What the badge does and doesn't cover

It covers three things: the automated checks pass, nothing is overdue for a look, and the review queue is no bigger than it was yesterday. It is not a verdict on the vault as a whole — and "no bigger than yesterday" is a low bar: the queue can grow steadily without ever tripping it. How complete the vault is, and how much is still being worked on, are separate measures below.

142 documents in the vault. The six cards above list 59 of them — the reusable building blocks. The other 83 are 22 benchmark write-ups, 20 rules and foundations, 17 reviews and proposals, 7 discovery and concept briefs, and 17 indexes, templates and generated files. The components themselves live in a different repo: 52 components, 12 of which have a written spec in this vault (23%), and 1 rendered in Storybook. Specs for the remaining 40 are the work after that.

Words used on this page

Contract — a written spec for one thing, so anyone building it makes the same choices. Nothing to do with commercial contracts.

Component — a reusable piece of interface: a button, a table, a dropdown.

Pattern — the shape of a whole page: a dashboard, a wizard, a list with a detail panel.

Storybook — a live site where every component is shown in every state. If a component isn't in there, its spec has to be written from source code alone, which is weaker evidence.

Agent — an AI teammate with one job and written instructions, like a builder or a reviewer.

Skill — a written procedure a person or an agent follows, step by step.

Review state — how settled a document is: draft, then in review, then approved.

Known mistake — a real error someone made before, written into the agent that made it so it doesn't happen twice.

Scored result — a recorded test where two versions of the same work were graded against a checklist.
