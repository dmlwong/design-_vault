# Concept brief: Enhancements to Risks, Issues, Decisions & Commentary (RAID)

**Requester:** Alexandre Blois · **Team:** Concept · **Submitted:** 2026-07-21

## A · Which corner of Efficio
- **Platform:** Both
- **Surface / feature area:** Other / shared

## B · The problem
- **Problem statement:** PMs cannot confidently manage risks, issues, decisions, actions, and commentary within CP today, so RAID-style tracking and status narrative often stay offline or in parallel tools. The current experience is hidden, modal-based, and largely free-text; commentary is not surfaced clearly, is hard to review across initiatives, and is not reused in status reporting or stakeholder outputs.
- **Why now / evidence:** PMs are already asking for more centralised, transparent, actionable ways to manage commentary and RAID; addressing it now improves confidence in CP as a live PM tool and reduces offline tracking. Initial evidence shows recurring demand for consolidated commentary, audit trails (who added/changed a note, when, and what changed), and surfaced risk mitigation.

## C · Who and what they do
- **Primary user + situation:** A Project Manager working across multiple initiatives in CP, trying to capture and review RAID and commentary without dropping to offline trackers.
- **Key journey / tasks:**
  1. PM opens an initiative and adds a risk, issue, decision, action, or commentary update via clear entry points
  2. PM reviews the latest surfaced commentary and RAID before a status meeting, filtering by type and seeing who added each and when
  3. PM updates multiple initiatives efficiently from a consolidated view
  4. PM reuses the captured commentary in Sigma and status outputs

## D · Success and edges
- **Key outcomes:** PMs have a clearer, faster, more trusted way to capture and use RAID and commentary in CP; reduced reliance on offline trackers; improved visibility of key project updates; and a reusable foundation for CP and CCP.
- **Out of scope / non-goals:** Not rebuilding the RAID/commentary/initiative status data model in one sprint; no full PMO tracker replacement, full audit-trail implementation, full Kanban/action system, or portfolio-level risk dashboard; no CP-only changes that create avoidable divergence from CCP.
- **Known constraints:** One-sprint scope (Sprint 83). Must be reusable for CCP or designed with CCP replication in mind. Existing commentary is modal-based and free-text; reuse into Sigma/status output may need reshaped or structured data (flagged unknown).

## E · What already exists
- **Existing material:** Sponsor Intake concept pack (Enhancements to Risks, Issues, Decisions & Commentary, Sprint 83). Current state: a hidden top-right modal split by last period / this period. No Lovable/Figma yet.
- **Open questions / assumptions you're unsure about:** Whether RAID types are separate or one update model with categories; which fields are required per type; internal-only vs client-visible handling; initiative-only vs milestone/task/deliverable level; whether a consolidated multi-initiative flow is feasible in one sprint.
